# Observable

## Overview

Observable is a declarative cognition-guidance artifact.

Observable is not a filter and not a centralized observability controller.

It exists to influence how agents compute perception policy.

## Purpose

Observable provides structured guidance about:
- useful signal sources
- observation priorities
- suggested frequency
- thresholds and escalation hints
- environmental or domain context

Observable influences perception but does not strictly control it.

## Relationship with Perception

Agents compute perception using multiple sources:
- native knowledge
- declaration data
- Observable guidance
- cognition history

Observable is therefore one influence among several.

## Scope

Observable may be associated with:
- cluster domains
- namespaces
- services
- infrastructure
- external systems
- operational concerns

## Future Direction

Observable is expected to become a CRD and a first-class cognition artifact in Hermes-on-K8s.

Its detailed schema and lifecycle remain future design work.

## Summary

Observable is a declarative perception-guidance artifact used to influence adaptive cognition in Hermes-on-K8s.
