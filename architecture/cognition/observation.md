# Observation

## Overview

Observation represents perception inside the Hermes collective.

Observations are usually derived from external substrates rather than created by Hermes.

Examples include:

- Kubernetes events
- logs
- metrics
- topology changes
- node conditions
- external system signals

## Purpose

Observations provide raw inputs to cognition.

They may trigger:

- Sessions
- Tasks
- reasoning
- escalation

## Ownership

Observations are not owned.

They are perceived by agents operating within their scope.
